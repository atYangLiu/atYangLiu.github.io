<div class="home-intro" markdown="1">
I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Agricultural and Consumer Economics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Gal Hochman. I study how energy and environmental policies shape economic outcomes across regions, sectors, firms, and households.

My research combines computable general equilibrium (CGE) modeling, input–output analysis, and applied econometrics, with a focus on energy transitions, agriculture, land use, and distributional consequences.
</div>
